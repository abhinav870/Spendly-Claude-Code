Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spendly, a personal expense tracking application, collects, uses, and protects your information. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the core functionality of Spendly, we collect a small amount of information that you provide directly when creating and using an account. This includes:

  • Account information — your username, email address, and a securely hashed password used to sign in.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions of the transactions you log.
  • Session data — standard technical information such as IP address and browser type, used to keep you signed in and protect your account.

We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, card details, or tax identifiers. Spendly is a manual tracking tool — you enter your own numbers, and we never ask for the rest.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information you provide is used only to operate, maintain, and improve Spendly. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Store and retrieve the expense records you create.
  • Generate the category breakdowns, summaries, and date-range views you request inside the app.
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to abuse, bugs, or security incidents.

We do not use your expense data to build advertising profiles, and we do not share it with marketers or data brokers of any kind.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a database associated with Spendly and is protected using industry-standard safeguards. Passwords are stored as one-way cryptographic hashes, so even our team cannot read them in plain text.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for using a strong, unique password.

You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and all associated records at any time by contacting us through the details below.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly is a small, self-contained application and does not rely on invasive third-party tracking. Where we do use third-party services — for example, a hosting provider or a basic analytics tool — we choose providers that respect your privacy and limit the data shared with them to what is strictly necessary to operate the service.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own purposes. If third-party data practices change in a way that affects you, we will update this section and, where appropriate, notify you through the service.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You remain in control of your information. At any time you can:

  • Sign in and review the expense data you have stored.
  • Update or correct your account details.
  • Request an export of your data.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records.

To exercise any of these rights, reach out using the contact information below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, our practices, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you through the service.

Continued use of Spendly after changes have been posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. If you do not agree to the updates, please discontinue use of the service or contact us to delete your account.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, want to request a copy of your data, or would like to close your account, please reach out through the contact information provided in the application. We take privacy questions seriously and will do our best to help.
